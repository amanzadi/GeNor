Methods, defaults and validation status¶

Each method lists its implementation, key defaults (with the reason), and how it is validated. The skills in skills/ explain interpretation and the decisions left to the user.

Summary statistics¶

genor sumstats load: reads TSV/CSV/space-delimited text (optionally gzip/zip), Parquet and single-trait GWAS-VCF. Columns are detected from ~150 aliases (src/genor/sumstats/columns.py), including PLINK 2 .glm (A1 is the effect allele), REGENIE, SAIGE (Allele2 is the effect allele), BOLT-LMM, METAL, FinnGen and GWAS Catalog harmonised files. --col canonical=FILECOLUMN overrides.

  • Odds ratios become log-odds; a beta column that looks like odds ratios stops with a question.
  • Z from beta/SE; SE from beta and P when SE is missing; P from Z when P is missing.
  • Build inferred from ~4,000 marker SNPs with known GRCh37/GRCh38 positions.
  • Individual-level files (.bed/.pgen/.bgen/..., VCFs with genotypes) are refused.

QC defaults: MAF ≥ 0.01 (LD panels of ~500 people do not tag rarer variants), INFO ≥ 0.8, valid P/SE/beta/alleles, duplicates resolved by smallest P. Palindromic SNPs and the MHC are flagged, not removed. Reports λGC, λ1000, mean χ², genome-wide significant count.

Liftover: native UCSC chain-file liftover for SNP positions (no external tool). Variants mapping to the reverse strand or another chromosome are dropped and counted.

Validation: unit tests for each format, the refusal path, build inference, QC counts, allele harmonisation (aligned, swapped, strand-flipped, palindromic resolved by frequency, dropped).

Meta-analysis (genor meta run)¶

Fixed-effect inverse-variance weighting (METAL SCHEME STDERR) with Cochran's Q, I² and DerSimonian-Laird random effects; or sample-size-weighted Z (METAL SCHEME SAMPLESIZE). Alleles are aligned to the first study reporting each variant.

Validation: identical to R meta::metagen (fixed, random, Q, I², τ²) to 1e-8 relative on a 60-variant, 3-study fixture.

LD score regression (genor ldsc h2, genor ldsc rg)¶

Regression code vendored unmodified from CBIIT/ldsc (Python 3 port of bulik/ldsc, GPL-3). Data handling reproduces munge_sumstats.py (SNVs only, no strand- ambiguous SNPs, INFO > 0.9, MAF > 0.01, N ≥ N90/1.5) and ldsc.py (200 blocks; two-step estimator at χ² 30 for h2; reference-panel SNP order). Reference: 1000G Phase 3 EUR HapMap3 LD scores and weights from Zenodo 10515792 (CC BY 4.0).

Liability-scale h2 uses the sample prevalence from case/control counts and the user's population prevalence. Warnings: mean χ² < 1.02, h2 z < 4, intercept > 1.1 or < 0.95, rg outside [-1.2, 1.2], cross-trait intercept ≠ 0 (sample overlap).

Validation: identical to the original ldsc.py --h2/--rg (h2, rg, SEs, intercepts to 1e-6 relative) on the same munged input (tests/test_ldsc_golden.py, marked slow: downloads 25 MB).

Clumping (genor clump run)¶

PLINK 2 --clump per chromosome against 1000G Phase 3 EUR or EAS. Presets: loci (p 5e-8, r² 0.1, 250 kb), mr (p 5e-8, r² 0.001, 10 Mb). --mode distance keeps the strongest variant per window without LD, always with a distance_clumping warning.

Mendelian randomisation (genor mr run)¶

Instrument selection (p < 5e-8, mr clumping), harmonisation (TwoSampleMR action 2: palindromes with MAF > 0.42 dropped), then: IVW (multiplicative random effects, main), IVW fixed effects, MR-Egger with intercept and I²_GX, weighted median (bootstrap SE), weighted and simple mode (bootstrap SE), Wald ratios, leave-one-out IVW, Cochran's Q (IVW and Egger), Steiger directionality, F statistics. Plots: scatter, forest, funnel, leave-one-out. STROBE-MR checklist with each of the 20 items marked filled / partial / human.

The analysis asks the user about sample overlap unless --overlap is given.

Validation against TwoSampleMR 0.7.14 on a 141-instrument fixture: IVW, IVW-FE, MR-Egger (slope, intercept, SEs, p, Q), Wald ratio and leave-one-out identical to 1e-8; weighted median and weighted/simple mode point estimates identical to 1e-6 (the mode uses a port of R's density()); bootstrap SEs agree within Monte Carlo error.

condFDR / conjFDR (genor pleio run)¶

GeNor aligns each trait to pleioFDR's 9,545,380-SNP template (z signed to the template A1), writes logpvec/zvec MAT files and config.txt, and runs the pleioFDR Python port (commit 45e03b9), which is itself validated against the MATLAB version. Defaults: condFDR threshold 0.01, conjFDR 0.05, 500 random prunings, MHC excluded.

Validation: a round trip on the chr21 demo data (GeNor alignment of re-encoded traits, half with swapped alleles, vs pleioFDR on the original MAT files) gives identical result tables (scripts/golden/pleio_roundtrip.py).

Fine-mapping and colocalisation¶

genor finemap abf: Wakefield approximate Bayes factors, one causal variant, prior 1e-4 per variant, prior effect SD 0.15·sdY (quantitative, sdY estimated from MAF and N as in coloc) or 0.2 (case-control); credible set conditional on a causal variant being present.

genor coloc abf: coloc ABF with p1 = p2 = 1e-4, p12 = 1e-5; reports PP.H0–H4 and per-SNP PP.H4.

Validation: identical to coloc::coloc.abf and coloc::finemap.abf (coloc 5.2.3) to 1e-6, both with supplied and estimated sdY.

Gene-level and lookups¶

  • MAGMA gene / gene-set / gene-property: drives a user-installed MAGMA (annotation window 35 kb up / 10 kb down by default).
  • Open Targets Platform GraphQL: search, target, disease, variant credible sets with L2G, colocalisations of a credible set.
  • Ensembl: VEP consequences (GRCh38 or GRCh37 server), genes overlapping a region.
  • STRING v12 (version-pinned URL, ≥ 1 s between requests): network and PPI enrichment.
  • g:Profiler: GO/Reactome/KEGG/HPA enrichment with g:SCS correction and optional custom background.

Validation: offline tests with recorded responses (tests/fixtures/http), and a nightly live job that checks the services still answer in the expected shape.
